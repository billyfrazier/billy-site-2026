# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
People looking for alternative, humorous career advice — students figuring out a first path, freelancers piecing together a living, and full-time employees quietly plotting an escape. They arrive from Billy's Substack, Medium, social posts, or a talk, in a browsing mood, deciding in seconds whether this person is worth their time. Secondary audience: event organizers and companies evaluating Billy as a speaker or collaborator.

## Product Purpose
The home base for Billy Frazier — writer, speaker, and author of *Fumbling Forward*. It exists to convert curious readers into book buyers (primary), and to route speaking/hiring enquiries and Substack subscriptions (secondary). Success = outbound "Get the book" clicks, Substack subscribes originating here, and contact-form submissions.

## Positioning
Career advice from someone honest enough to admit he's still figuring it out — and funny enough that you'll keep reading.

## Conversion & proof
- Primary CTA: **Get the book** → fumblingbook.com / retailers.
- Secondary CTA: subscribe to the Substack (fumblingforward.substack.com); tertiary: the contact form (book a talk / hire / ask a question).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *"Fumbling forward through work and life while sharing notes with the rest of the class."*
- Belief ladder: (1) this person is funny and honest → (2) he's actually lived this, five career stages of it → (3) the book is the concentrated version → (4) buying it is a small, easy yes.
- Proof on hand: the book itself (out now, five retailers); self-aware joke blurbs (author's inner monologue, his mum); Substack + Medium body of writing. Real photos pending — placeholders until Billy supplies them.

## Brand Personality
Self-deprecating, honest, energetic. The site should make you smile within the first screen and trust him by the second. Confident craft delivering unconfident-on-purpose copy — the design is polished so the humility reads as a choice, not a shortfall.

## Anti-references
- Corporate LinkedIn-guru energy: navy suits, "thought leader", stat counters.
- Sterile minimalist portfolio: all whitespace, no jokes, no color.
- Hustle-bro landing page: countdown timers, highlighter yellow, "join 50,000 winners".

## Design Principles
1. **The joke is in the copy, the confidence is in the craft.** Humor never excuses sloppy design.
2. **One page, one path.** Every section nudges toward the book; nothing competes with it.
3. **Type is the imagery** until real photos arrive — scale and rhythm do the visual work.
4. **Borrow structure, not identity.** Reference sites (annamackenzie.com, brunocis.co, springsummer.dk, jamesclear.com, lisa.locomotive.ca) inform scale, pacing, and clarity — never cloned.
5. **Fast and free.** Static output, no paid services, nothing that slows the page down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: body contrast ≥4.5:1, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation.
